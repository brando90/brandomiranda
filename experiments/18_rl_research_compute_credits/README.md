## Turn credits into a useful research request

The proposed grant pitch should fund one bounded, reproducible study: **test whether environment-selection measurements improve decisions on independently assembled candidate environments under a fixed total compute budget**. Include simple fixed-choice and crossover baselines, repeated random seeds, a frozen evaluation protocol and all probing/calibration costs. Promise a useful benchmark and honest positive or negative results, not an unestablished allocation advantage.

A practical first request is **$5,000 of training-instance credits**, with a larger **$10,000** staged proposal where supported. Specify the hardware, maximum concurrent devices, expected experiment count, storage, evaluation and restart allowance. These are proposed request sizes, not approved spending or measured project costs. Keep confidential manuscript details and unpublished results out of public application materials unless their release is intended.

For scale, Modal's current B200 GPU-task price is **$0.001736/second = $6.2496/GPU-hour**. At that rate, $5,000 buys at most about **800 B200 GPU-hours**, and $10,000 about **1,600**, before processors, memory, storage, network charges or pricing modifiers. This is an illustrative conversion, not a Lambda quote or a guarantee of hardware availability. [Pricing](https://modal.com/pricing).

Modal supports up to eight B200/H100 GPUs on one machine; more than two can increase waits. Functions default to five minutes and permit up to 24 hours, so long training jobs need an explicit timeout. GPU jobs remain preemptible: the paid non-preemptible option is not supported for GPU Functions. Test full training-state recovery or budget whole-run restarts. Use the workspace's out-of-pocket spending limit as well as the usage budget. [Hardware](https://modal.com/docs/guide/gpu), [timeouts](https://modal.com/docs/guide/timeouts), [preemption](https://modal.com/docs/guide/preemption), [budgets](https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets).
