2/
Everyone. F# minor seventh over a B bass is B9sus4: F# is the fifth, not the third, and E is the suspended fourth in both books. Nobody has a third. With no third, the same seven notes of E major can be named from B (Mixolydian), F# (Dorian), or E (the key). All three are right.

(279 characters)

3/
The pentatonics fall out of the same fact. Inside the parent scale, E major, there are three major pentatonics: on the root, the fourth, and the flat seventh. Only the root one has the third. So "E pent, A pent" (both major) and my note "1 4 b7" say the same thing.

(265 characters)

4/
The Lydian-dominant rule is real, just about a different chord. One question settles it: does anyone want the natural fourth? The chord wants it (sus): Mixolydian. The key wants it (a V7 going home): Mixolydian. Nobody wants it: Lydian dominant. A sus chord never even asks.

(274 characters)
